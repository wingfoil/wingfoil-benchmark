We run a windsurf school on the lake, and we rent boards and sails to our students and to visitors. We
would like the booking desk to have a rental module, starting with the basics.

Please export `createRentalService()` from `src/index.ts`. It returns a service that keeps everything in
memory, with:

- `addEquipment(item)`, where `item` is `{ id, kind, dailyPrice }`: `id` is a string, `kind` is `'board'`
  or `'sail'`, and `dailyPrice` is what one day of rental costs;
- `quote(request)`, where `request` is `{ itemId, start, end }`, which returns
  `{ itemId, start, end, total }`: what renting that item for that period costs;
- `book(request)`, with the same request, which books the item and returns
  `{ id, itemId, start, end, total }`, where `id` identifies the booking.

`quote` and `book` throw an error for an unknown item or a period the rules below do not allow, and
`book` also throws when the item is not free for the whole period.

These are the school's rules, and they apply to everything the module does:

1. Money is an integer number of euro cents.
2. Times are in UTC, written as ISO-8601 strings.
3. A booking covers whole days only: a period starts and ends at midnight UTC, and it ends at the start
   of the day after its last day.
4. The same item cannot be booked twice for overlapping periods.
5. Cancellation policy: a booking cancelled less than 24 hours before its start gets no refund;
   otherwise the full amount is refunded.

Do not implement cancellations yet. The cancellation policy will be needed later.
