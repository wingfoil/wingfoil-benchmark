We want the app to show how old a task is, and what changed recently. Please record when each task was
created and when it was last changed.

Every task has `createdAt` and `updatedAt`, as ISO-8601 date-time strings. When a task is added the two
are the same. Every change to a task (completing, reopening, renaming, tagging or untagging it) sets its
`updatedAt` to the time of the change.
