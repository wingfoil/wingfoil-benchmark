Teams are moving to Tidy Tasks from their spreadsheets. Please add a public helper for bulk import:
`importTasks(list, text)`, exported from `src/index.ts`.

The text holds one task per line, as `title;priority;tags`: the priority may be left empty for a normal
one, and the tags are separated by spaces, for example `Renew the insurance;high;admin money`. Blank lines
are skipped.

It adds every valid line to the list and returns `{ imported, rejected }`: `imported` is how many tasks
were added, and `rejected` lists every line that could not be added as `{ line, reason }`, with `line`
counting from 1.
