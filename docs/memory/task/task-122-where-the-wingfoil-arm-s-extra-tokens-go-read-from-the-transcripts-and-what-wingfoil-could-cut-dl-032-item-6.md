## Context

[dl-032](../decision-log/dl-032-a-weak-model-with-wingfoil-against-a-strong-model-without-it-cost-and-time-at-equal-quality-and-a-local-model-s-estimate.md) item 6: read the wingfoil arm's transcripts for what its extra tokens are, and file
what WingFoil could cut as feedback notes (the inbox, `docs/wingfoil-feedback/`, dl-163); every token saved weighs
most on a local model. [task-121](../task/task-121-weak-with-a-harness-against-strong-without-the-cross-model-parity-comparison-and-a-local-model-s-estimate-dl-032.md)'s parity section made the question concrete: on campaign-008
(Claude Code), Haiku 5.5 with wingfoil matches Sonnet 5.5's baseline in quality at 0.27–0.44× the cost but takes
3.8–6.3× the time, re-reading 6.8–16× the cached tokens; on campaign-007 (Codex) Luna with wingfoil holds H-W on S1
at 2.0× the cached tokens. The approver asked for it in chat (2026-11-01). Placed in v0.3, W17. A reading of stored
transcripts only: no real-agent session, 0 €; nothing written into WingFoil (dl-009, dl-163).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A breakdown, generated by a script** over the stored transcripts of campaign-008 (`33b54dd89e8b/1`, both
   models) and campaign-007 (`697e79b75e41/1`, both models): per run and arm, the tokens of each turn attributed to
   what caused the turn and what it read, at least: the manual and the session's context; WingFoil commands and
   their output (`workflow next`/`status`, `memory search`, `directives list`, the verbs), by command; documents read
   (Memory elements, directives); the stop at a gate and the resume after the approver's reply (a new session's
   context reloaded); the scenario's own work (code, tests). The wingfoil arm against its docs control and the
   baseline on the same scenario and model, so that the extra is the difference. Reproducible from `results/` and the
   git-ignored transcripts; the script in the repository (`scripts/`), its output in a calibration note.
2. **The reading:** which components account for most of the extra on each agent and model, per gate and per step;
   what scales with the turns (context re-read) and what is fixed (a gate's round trip); a comparison with task-121's
   ratios (cached tokens, time) and with campaign-008's cost by component (its Results review).
3. **Feedback notes:** for each cut WingFoil could make (a command's output too long, a document re-read each step,
   a resume that reloads more than needed, directives listed in full), an `F-<next>` note in
   `docs/wingfoil-feedback/` with its ledger row, written generically (reproducible with WingFoil alone, no name of
   this repository), with the measured share it would save; none when the reading finds none. Filing in WingFoil
   stays the approver's (dl-009).
4. A note in `docs/calibration/` (or a section of `v0.3.md`) with the breakdown's tables and the reading, figures
   generated; the method page untouched unless a reading changes what it states.
5. Checks: prettier, `tsc`, eslint (the script), `scripts/pre-review.sh main`; the script's own test (a small
   transcript fixture); the full suites on the approver's go; an independent review (dl-020).
