## Context

The fourth W16 task of [plan-005](../../plans/plan-005-release-v0-3.md), its first half (the approver's choice A in
chat, 2026-11-03: S1 1.1 here; S2, S3 and S8 1.1 with the v2 checks and rubrics in the next task):
[bug-025](../bug/bug-025-s1-1-0-s-step-5-leaves-createpatch-s-diff-ambiguous-and-its-result-is-bimodal-in-every-arm.md).
S1 1.0's step 5 asks only that a patch "describe what changed", while its pairs' length bound, set from a reference
that diffs arrays by their longest common run, in effect asks for a shift-aware diff; every arm's result is bimodal
by the diff the agent chooses. The scenario conventions' K7 (amendment 1.5) state the versions 1.1, and K6 that each
1.1 records whether its 1.0 was saturated; no requirement changes (requirements 1.49's note, task-126). No real-agent
session, no spending.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Red-first, the drafted scenario** "S1 1.1's step 5 has one reading" (`scenarios.feature` @F3.4): S1 1.1 loads and
   passes the validator and the leak scan; its step 5's request adds the approver's sentence (choice D, 2026-11-03):
   "When an array gains or loses an item, the patch adds or removes that item, and does not rewrite the items around
   it."; S1 1.0 stays registered, unchanged, with its limit stated on the method page.
2. **S1 1.1 is 1.0 but for that sentence:** its seed, its other prompts, its oracle (suites, pairs, applier, licences)
   and its arm files are byte-identical to 1.0's (a test compares the trees), its `version` is `1.1`, so its hash is
   its own (REQ-FMT-09).
3. **The reference passes:** S1's reference (`test/fixtures/reference/S1`) scores the maximum on S1 1.1's public
   suites after step 5, as on 1.0's.
4. **K6's record:** `S1.md` gains an amendment for 1.1 stating the sentence and whether 1.0 was saturated, read by a
   script from the published campaigns (every arm at the maximum of its final public hidden tests in every run), the
   figures quoted from its output.
5. **The hold-out** (the approver's choice C): S1 1.0's hold-out additions copied, unchanged, to
   `scenarios/S1/1.1/` of the private hold-out repository, by the agent once the approver gives its path in chat,
   nothing of it read or printed; the validator with the hold-out configured passes on 1.1. Until then the criterion
   is open and the task does not go to review.
6. **The method page** states S1 1.0's step-5 limit (`#s1-step-5-limit`): its results are bimodal by the diff the agent
   chose, stay as published, and S1 1.1 states the diff.
7. Checks: prettier on every changed file, `tsc`, eslint, `scripts/pre-review.sh main`; an independent review
   (dl-020); the full suites on the approver's go.
