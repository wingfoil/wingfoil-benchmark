## 8. The hypotheses, restated on Sonnet 5.5

[v0.1.md](v0.1.md) §11's hypotheses compare the wingfoil arm on WingFoil with its workflow engine against v0.2.2 on
"the same model and agent". In v0.2 that pair is the reference campaign (v0.3.0) against the bridge (v0.2.2), both on
Sonnet 5.5 at `medium` with Claude Code 2.1.300: the hypotheses read that model. They are not read on Opus 5.5: its
slice runs S1 in three arms of the reference campaign only, and the bridge has no slice, so no v0.3.0 → v0.2.2 pair
exists on it; the slice reads the frontier's other end (§4: every arm 165/165, 48/48 at 4–6 times Sonnet's cost). Their "Holds when" is unchanged; the
values below are calibration's (n = 1), to be replaced by the campaigns'. WingFoil v0.3.0's arm declares
`workflow-engine: true`, but its scaffold declares no workflow and no step asks for one (task-095): the engine stays
unstarted, so H1, H2 and H8, the engine's own, are expected to fail on S1–S3 and S8, as the arm measures WingFoil as
its documentation sets it up.
