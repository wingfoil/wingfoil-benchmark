([dl-006](../memory/decision-log/dl-006-real-agent-runs-during-delivery-are-declared-by-the-workflow.md)).

records it; calibration's dry runs, validation's run and the campaign add theirs in their phases
(`release-cycle`).

nothing is billed per token (sequencer decision 1). EUR at the rate the run's campaign declares, or,
for a spike, not converted. **None of the delivery runs is a dry run**: calibration measures its own.
