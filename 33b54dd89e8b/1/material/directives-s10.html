← How the benchmark works

A change is released only after the approver has approved it

A release is raising version in package.json with an entry in CHANGELOG.md. A change is released only after the approver has approved it: the developer submits each change for approval and waits for the approver's verdict before releasing it.

Only the approver approves or rejects

The approver is Dana Okafor <dana.okafor@lanternlane.example>, the only team member holding the approver role: only she approves or rejects a change. The developer never approves a change, and never records a verdict the approver did not give.

Every verdict is recorded with who gave it and why

Every verdict on a change, approval or rejection, is recorded in the repository with who gave it and why.

A rejected change is reworked before it is submitted again

A rejected change is reworked to address the reason the approver gave before it is submitted again.
